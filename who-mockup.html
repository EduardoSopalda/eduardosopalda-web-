01 / The throughline

More than twenty years.
One recurring question.

How do we make complex systems work for people—and keep responsibility human?

For more than twenty years, I’ve worked across solution architecture, global customer experience, technology operations, data quality, and governance. Different roles; one pattern: connect the technical system to the people who depend on it.

My work has taken me from Barcelona to Tokyo, from Lima to New York. Across markets and disciplines, I keep returning to the same things: clear meaning, accountable systems, and trust earned in practice.

Global experience · Barcelona / Tokyo / Lima / New York

Networks & people

I started in technical data analysis and incident operations for enterprise networks, working between customers and engineers across Europe and Asia, where reliability had to hold under pressure.

Architecture

A different kind of systems thinking: how parts fit, how people move through spaces, and how a plan meets real constraints.

Trust & AI quality

Trust & Safety, process optimization, and human-in-the-loop AI across multilingual teams. The work made feedback matter: a quality initiative raised KPIs by 15 points.

Governance

03 / Outside the title · Still learning. That part doesn’t stop.

People are the point.
Curiosity is the method.

I’ve spent years close to the operational reality of technology: the customer waiting for a fix, the team interpreting a policy, the person expected to trust an AI system. That is why the work has to make sense to people, not only on paper.

I contribute to DAMA España’s AI and Data Governance Working Group, write about the questions I meet in practice, and keep learning by building—most recently, exploring agentic AI from the inside.

“Data is our greatest asset.”
It’s a good line. It’s also wrong.
What’s actually greatest: people.
